The formula, and what changed since the first pass

S = 0.15 × conf + 0.30 × hbond + 0.25 × planarity + 0.15 × cat_polarity + 0.15 × apolar_match. Geometry therefore carries 0.85 against the shipped benchmark's 0.50.

hbond — the pipeline's own geometry composite, distance and acceptor-side angle.

planarity — how close each donor sits to the plane of the ligand's aromatic system. All three specified contacts are to sp2 lone pairs lying in that plane, so a side chain parked over the ring face can be 2.9 å from its acceptor and still unable to engage it. Distance alone cannot tell the two apart.

cat_polarity — whether the residues lining the triazole and nitro end of the pocket are polar.

apolar_match — whether the unsubstituted benzo edge is lined with apolar residues rather than charge.

The restored designs. The first pass skipped every fastrelax-* run. Those runs wrote term_distance / term_angle under weights conf 0.70 / geom 0.30, while the later runs write term_catalytic / term_non_catalytic / term_angle under conf 0.50 / geom 0.50. old_geometry() looked only for the newer column names, so on a fastrelax row it matched term_angle alone and returned the angle as the whole geometry composite, dropping the distance. Running with --include-legacy alone would have inflated those designs. Here the composite is rebuilt as 0.6 × distance + 0.4 × angle at that run's own weights.

Still not perfectly uniform. The hbond slice is each row's own pipeline definition. The confidence term is identical across both configs (complex_ipde ramped 0.5 to 5.0 å), and planarity, cat_polarity and apolar_match are recomputed from the structures the same way for every design. So roughly 70% of the score is strictly comparable and 30% approximately so.

Two designs are absent. 02.c01.wiggle.4b0d78 and 02.c01.wiggle.6d5166 have no committed structure — Boltz served them from cache as duplicates of 4642b0, so nothing was written to re-score.
