Why 232 and not 205. The first rerank pass skipped every fastrelax-* run, dropping 29 designs including all 12 of variant 12's hits, because the two run families write different score columns. The rescored round fixes that and covers every scored design with a committed structure — 142 depth, 63 overnight, 27 fastrelax. Two of variant 02's fastrelax hits have no structure in the repo and so are absent.

Adjustments are not the cycle number. The search re-expands the seed every cycle, so a design labelled c03 may descend from its seed through only one or two actual adjustments — true for 40 of the 232. The filter above counts the real parent chain, which is what the filename encodes.

The dashed lines are measured here, not read from a table. For each design the bond's two atoms are taken from the rerank's own catalytic_residues mapping — lig_n3_to_asp229_od2=ASP232 means the target's ASP229 is residue 232 in this design — and the distance is computed straight from the deposited coordinates. All 671 bonds across the 232 designs resolved. Green to 3.5 Å, amber to the 4.5 Å fail threshold, red beyond.

Reading the geometry. Offset is how far the partner atom sits out of the ligand's aromatic plane; the angle is between the partner's own functional-group plane and that same plane. Both matter, and they fail independently: a partner can sit perfectly in-plane while its lone pairs point out of it. Offsets are marked green under 1 Å, amber to the script's 2 Å failure point, red beyond.

What this cannot tell you. Every number here comes from a single Boltz prediction. The repo's own noise control measured seed-to-seed spread on one fixed sequence at ±4 Å and ±28°, and both batches ran with eval_replicates: 1. Differences smaller than that are not resolvable. The rerank's own caveat, from its JSON: one reference structure, and cat_polarity was revised after seeing it — "a consistency check, not a validation."
